1. _____ is considered as one of the most important mechanical inventions of all times.
Answer: Wheel

Answer: Mesopotamia, 5500
3. A wheel is a _____ component that can rotate on its centre.
Answer: circular
4. Wheel is a _____ that enables efficient movement of an _____ across a surface where there is a force pressing it to the surface.
Answer: device, object
5. The coefficient of friction at the interface of wheel is usually _____.
Answer: lower
6. How many years ago was the wheel invented? (MCQ)
Answer: (c) 5000 years
7. Spoked wheel is used for which category of vehicle? (MCQ)
Answer: (a) Light
8. Axle of wheel is fitted in the wheel in the _____. (MCQ)
Answer: (c) centre
9. Define a wheel's structure. (Short Answer)
Answer: Early wheels were simple wooden disks with a hole for the axle. A wheel is a circular component that can rotate on its centre, facilitating motion by rolling together with the use of axles.
10. List the different types of wheel used in transport with examples. (Short Answer)
Answer: 1. Early Wooden Wheels 2. Spoked Wheels(Used in bicycle and Motor Cycles) 3. Locomotive Wheels (Used in train) 4. Trolley Wheels (Small rubber/ Plastic wheels used in bags and trolleys).

Key Points & Facts

  • Invented: Mesopotamia (modern-day Iraq) around 3500 BC.
  • Age: Approx. 5500 years old.
  • First Use: Potter's wheel (4500 BC), then transportation (3200 BC).
  • Spoked Wheel: Middle Bronze Age; lighter & swifter vehicles.
  • Function: Reduces friction by rolling motion.

বাংলা ইঞ্জিন এমন একটি যন্ত্র যেখানে জ্বালানি পুড়িয়ে শক্তি উৎপন্ন হয়। এটি জ্বালানির রাসায়নিক শক্তিকে যান্ত্রিক শক্তিতে রূপান্তরিত করে গাড়ি চালায়। পিস্টন, সিলিন্ডার, ক্র্যাঙ্কশ্যাফট ও ভালভ একসাথে কাজ করে দহন ঘটায় ও শক্তি সঞ্চালন করে।
11. Explain the difference between compression ignition engine and spark ignition engine. (Short Answer)

বাংলা SI ইঞ্জিনে পেট্রোল ও বাতাসের মিশ্রণ স্পার্ক প্লাগের বৈদ্যুতিক স্পার্কে জ্বলে ওঠে। CI ইঞ্জিনে শুধু বাতাস সংকুচিত হয়ে উচ্চ চাপ ও তাপমাত্রায় পৌঁছায়, তখন ডিজেল ইনজেক্ট করলে সেই তাপেই জ্বলে ওঠে।

Key Points & Facts

  • IC Engine: Fuel burned inside (Internal Combustion).
  • SI Engine: Petrol; spark plug ignition.
  • CI Engine: Diesel; heat of compression (up to 538°C).
  • Major Parts: Cylinder, Piston, Crankshaft, Connecting Rod.
  • Cylinder Block: Main body; withstands ~70 bar pressure and ~700°C temp.
  • Carburettor: Mixes air and fuel (petrol engines).
  • Crankshaft: Backbone of engine; converts reciprocating to rotary motion.

9. How does a cooling system work? (Short Answer)
Answer: Liquid coolant circulates through water jackets surrounding the engine cylinder block, absorbing excess heat from cylinder walls through conduction . When coolant temperature reaches 71°C to 82°C , the thermostat valve opens, directing hot coolant to the radiator where heat is dissipated into ambient air. If temperatures exceed 82°C , the electric/belt-driven cooling fan switches on to increase airflow.
বাংলা কুল্যান্ট সিলিন্ডারের চারপাশের ওয়াটার জ্যাকেটে ঘুরে পরিচলন (Conduction) প্রক্রিয়ায় তাপ শোষণ করে। তাপমাত্রা ৭১°C–৮২°C হলে থার্মোস্ট্যাট ভালভ খুলে কুল্যান্টকে রেডিয়েটরে পাঠায়, যেখানে তাপ বাতাসে ছড়িয়ে পড়ে। ৮২°C-এর ওপরে কুলিং ফ্যান চালু হয়ে রেডিয়েটরকে দ্রুত ঠান্ডা হতে সাহায্য করে।
10. What is the role of coolants? (Short Answer)
Answer: Coolant absorbs heat from engine components and transfers it to the radiator for dissipation. It prevents engine overheating in summer , prevents freezing in winter (due to antifreeze additives), and protects internal metal passages from rust and corrosion .
বাংলা কুল্যান্টের কাজ হলো ইঞ্জিনের অতিরিক্ত তাপ শোষণ করে রেডিয়েটরে নিয়ে যাওয়া। এটি গরমে ইঞ্জিনকে অতিরিক্ত গরম হওয়া এবং শীতে জমে যাওয়া থেকে রক্ষা করে (অ্যান্টিফ্রিজ উপাদানের জন্য)। এছাড়া এটি ইঞ্জিনের ভেতরে মরিচা ও ক্ষয় রোধ করে।

Key Points & Facts

  • Thermostat: Opens at 71°C–82°C.
  • Cooling Fan: Switches on above 82°C.
  • Heat Transfer: Conduction (cylinder wall → water jacket).
  • Motorcycle: Air cooling; cars — liquid cooling.
  • Coolant: Antifreeze — anti-freeze, anti-boil, anti-corrosion.

2. Differentiate between direct and indirect objects. (Short Answer)

Session 6: Pronunciation Basics

1. What is pronunciation? Why is correct pronunciation important? (Short Answer)
Answer: Pronunciation is the way you say a word , just as spelling is how you write a word. Correct pronunciation helps you express yourself in a clear and confident manner and helps others understand your words easily.
2. What is phonetics? (Short Answer)
Answer: Phonetics is the study of the sounds that we make when we speak . Every word is made up of one or more sounds put together (e.g., 'dog' = d-o-g).
3. English has 26 letters but how many sounds? (Short Answer)
Answer: We use 26 letters to write English, but more than 26 sounds when we speak — this is why a word's spelling does not always match its pronunciation. Example: the letter 'a' sounds differently in cat (ऐ), car (आ) and call (ऑ).
4. What are the three basic types of sounds in English words? (Short Answer)
Answer: 1. Vowels — five vowels (a, e, i, o, u) but 12 vowel sounds.
2. Diphthongs — combination sound of two vowels; starts as one vowel sound and goes to another.
3. Consonants — any sound that is neither a vowel nor a diphthong.
5. Sometimes words with different spellings are pronounced the same. Give examples. (Short Answer)
Answer: Sun/Son, Sail/Sale, Tail/Tale — different spellings, same pronunciation. The best way to learn correct pronunciation is to listen carefully .

Key Points & Facts

  • Pronunciation: Way you SAY a word.
  • Phonetics: Study of speech sounds.
  • 26 letters but more than 26 sounds.
  • Sound types: Vowels (12 sounds), Diphthongs, Consonants.
  • 'a' sounds: cat (ऐ), car (आ), call (ऑ).

Part A: Role of brake.
Answer: The main role of a brake is to stop or slow down a moving vehicle safely within the shortest possible distance by converting kinetic energy into heat energy through friction.
বাংলা ব্রেকের প্রধান কাজ হলো ঘর্ষণের মাধ্যমে গতিশক্তিকে তাপশক্তিতে রূপান্তর করে চলন্ত গাড়ির গতি কমানো বা নিরাপদে থামানো।
Part B1: Importance of brakes in an automobile.
Answer: Brakes provide essential vehicle control, prevent road accidents, hold parked vehicles stationary, and safeguard passenger life.
বাংলা দুর্ঘটনা প্রতিরোধ করতে, পার্কিং অবস্থায় গাড়িকে স্থির রাখতে এবং যাত্রীদের নিরাপত্তা দিতে ব্রেক অত্যন্ত গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।
Part B2: Principle of brakes for automobiles.
Answer: Brakes operate on the principle of friction. When brake shoes or pads press against a rotating drum or disc, friction resists rotation and converts kinetic energy into thermal energy.
বাংলা ব্রেক ঘর্ষণ (Friction) নীতির ওপর কাজ করে। ব্রেক প্যাড বা শু চাকার ড্রাম বা ডিস্কের সাথে ঘর্ষণে লিপ্ত হয়ে গতিশক্তিকে তাপশক্তিতে পরিণত করে গাড়ি থামায়।
Part B3: Properties of good brakes.
Answer:
  1. Must bring vehicle to rest in the shortest distance.
  2. Must perform reliably in all weather conditions.
  3. Must require minimal pedal effort and minimal maintenance.
  4. Must not affect steering stability upon application.
বাংলা ভালো ব্রেকের গুণাবলী: দ্রুত গাড়ি থামানোর ক্ষমতা, সব আবহাওয়ায় কার্যকর হওয়া, কম চাপে কাজ করা এবং স্টিয়ারিং স্থায়িত্ব নষ্ট না করা।

14. Make a list of steps required to measure the clearance between piston and cylinder wall. (Activity)
S.No. Step
1 Clean the piston and cylinder bore thoroughly to remove all oil, dirt and carbon deposits.
2 Measure the inside diameter (ID) of the cylinder bore using a cylinder bore gauge or inside micrometer at the top, middle and bottom thrust faces.

Session 4: Testing of Cooling System and Replacement of Defective Components

1. List the common faults in a cooling system. (Short Answer)
Answer: Loose or broken water pump pulley belt; low coolant level ; faulty thermostat; faulty water pump; dirty or bent radiator fins; broken water pump fan; coolant leakage ; defective cooling fan motor.
2. Why is the cooling system important in an IC engine? (Short Answer)
Answer: Combustion in an IC engine reaches temperatures of 2000°C–2500°C . The cooling system is critical because:
• It prevents component damage — high heat breaks down engine oil, removing the protective oil film and causing severe friction.
• It prevents pre-ignition — overheated chambers cause fuel to ignite prematurely (knocking).
• It maintains efficiency — keeps the engine at its ideal temperature ( 80°C to 90°C ) for optimal power and fuel economy.
3. Name the different components of a cooling system. (Short Answer)
Answer: Radiator (with pressure cap and expansion/recovery tank), water pump (centrifugal), thermostat valve , cooling fan (mechanical or electric), water jackets , coolant hoses and clamps, and engine coolant (antifreeze mixture).
4. Name the different methods of engine cooling. (Short Answer)
Answer: Air cooling (fins, motorcycles/small engines); Water / Liquid cooling (radiator, standard in cars); Oil cooling (supplementary, high-performance engines).
5. Differentiate between the oil cooling system and the water cooling system. (Short Answer)
Water-Cooling System Oil Cooling System
Uses a mixture of distilled water and ethylene glycol (coolant). Uses engine lubricating oil.
Cools the entire cylinder block, cylinder head and engine assembly. Cools internal moving parts (pistons, bearings) and keeps oil viscosity stable.
High heat absorption capacity (high specific heat of water). Lower heat capacity compared to water.
Radiator, water pump, thermostat, water jackets, fan. Oil cooler (heat exchanger), oil pump, oil galleries.
Primary cooling method for cars and heavy vehicles. Supplementary cooling in air-cooled / high-stress engines.
6. What is the function of the radiator in a cooling system? (Short Answer)
Answer: The radiator acts as a heat exchanger : it receives hot coolant from the engine, passes it through thin metal tubes surrounded by cooling fins to transfer heat to outside air, and supplies cooled coolant back to the engine via the water pump.
7. Why is coolant added to the radiator instead of plain water? (Short Answer)
Answer: Coolant (50/50 distilled water + glycol-based antifreeze) has a higher boiling point and lower freezing point (prevents cracking of block/radiator in cold), provides corrosion protection (rust, scale), and lubricates the water pump seals and bearings.
8. What is the function of the thermostat? (Short Answer)
Answer: It is a temperature-sensitive valve : on a cold engine it stays closed (coolant bypasses radiator → fast warm-up); at operating temperature ( 80°C–90°C ) it opens , sending hot coolant to the radiator.
9. What are the functions of the water pump and cooling fan? (Short Answer)
Answer: Water pump: Forcibly circulates coolant through the water jackets, hoses and radiator whenever the engine runs.
Cooling fan: Draws ambient air through the radiator fins for continuous heat transfer — especially when the vehicle is stationary or at low speeds.

Session 5: Regular Servicing of MPFI System

1. What is the full form of MPFI? (Short Answer)
Answer: MPFI stands for Multi-Point Fuel Injection .
2. What are the main components of MPFI? (Short Answer)
Answer: Engine Control Module (ECM): The central brain of the system.
Sensors: MAF, Throttle Position Sensor (TPS), Coolant Temperature Sensor (CTS), Crankshaft Position Sensor, Oxygen Sensor, Knock Sensor.
Actuators: Fuel injectors, Idle Air Control (IAC) valve, fuel pump relay, ignition coils.
Fuel supply system: Electric fuel pump, filter, fuel rail, pressure regulator.
3. What are the advantages of MPFI over a conventional (carburettor) system? (Short Answer)
Answer: Equal fuel distribution directly into each cylinder's intake port; better fuel efficiency ; lower emissions ; instant acceleration .
4. What are the disadvantages of MPFI over a conventional system? (Short Answer)
Answer: Higher initial cost (complex electronics); complex troubleshooting (needs diagnostic scanners, trained technicians); costly replacement of sensors and ECM modules.
5. What precautions should be taken while working on an MPFI system? (Short Answer)
Answer: Depressurize fuel lines before disconnecting pipes/injectors; disconnect battery ground (−) before removing ECM connectors or sensors; avoid voltage spikes — never disconnect battery terminals while the engine is running; keep clean — prevent dirt entering fuel rails/injector tips and always replace sealing O-rings.

Session 6: Servicing of CRDI and Non-CRDI System

1. What is the full form of CRDI? (Short Answer)
Answer: CRDI stands for Common Rail Direct Injection .
2. What are the main components of CRDI? (Short Answer)
Answer: High-Pressure Fuel Pump (up to 1,500–2,500+ bar); Common Rail (accumulator storing fuel for all injectors); Rail Pressure Sensor & Regulator ; Fuel Injectors (solenoid or piezo, inject directly into cylinders); ECM & Sensors (manage injection timing and duration).
3. What are the advantages of CRDI over a conventional diesel system? (Short Answer)
Answer: Constant high pressure regardless of engine speed/load; multiple injections per cycle (pilot, main, post — reduces knock and noise); lower emissions & better efficiency (fine atomization, complete combustion); smoother operation (reduces NVH).
4. What are the disadvantages of CRDI over a conventional diesel system? (Short Answer)
Answer: High component cost (injectors, pumps, ECM); fuel quality sensitivity (contaminated fuel/water destroys injectors and pumps); complex diagnostics (needs computerized scanners and special tools).
5. What precautions should be taken while working on a CRDI system? (Short Answer)
Answer: Beware of extreme pressure — never loosen high-pressure lines while running or immediately after shutdown (wait for rail pressure to bleed down); maintain absolute cleanliness — wipe fittings before opening, micro-particles clog precision nozzles; electrical safety — disconnect battery negative terminal before working on ECM wiring or injector solenoids.
6. Explain the use of a turbocharger. (Short Answer)
Answer: A turbocharger uses exhaust gas to push extra air into the engine cylinders. This burns more fuel, significantly increasing engine power and torque without making the engine bigger.

2. Explain the various parts of the propeller shaft / drive shaft. (Short Answer)

  • CV Joints: Inboard (differential side) & outboard (wheel side).
  • Lubrication: U-joints & slip joint greased to avoid squeaking.
  • Bent Shaft Symptom: Noisy operation.

Session 3: Servicing of Differential Unit and Adjustments

1. What is the function of the differential unit? (Short Answer)
Answer: It redirects drive torque through 90° to drive the half axles, and allows the driving wheels to rotate at different speeds during cornering.
2. What is the importance and working of the differential? (Short Answer)
Answer: The crown wheel and pinion provide final speed reduction (increasing torque) and turn the drive through 90° ; the star gears allow the outer wheel to rotate faster than the inner wheel while turning, preventing tyre scrub. (Refer to Class IX & X notes for detailed working.)
3. What are the main parts of a differential? (Short Answer)
Answer: Bevel drive pinion , crown wheel (ring gear), differential carrier/cage , star gears (planet pinions), sun gears (side gears), half axle shafts , and thrust washers/brass shims.
4. Write the steps to adjust crown wheel and pinion clearance. (Short Answer)
Answer: Apply red oxide / Prussian blue paste on crown wheel teeth and rotate the pinion to check the contact pattern.
• Heavy face contact: Remove shims behind bevel pinion (move closer to crown wheel).
• Heavy flank contact: Add shims behind bevel pinion.
• Heavy toe/heel contact: Move shims between left and right crown wheel bearings.
• Measure backlash with a dial gauge on a crown wheel tooth (permissible 0.15 mm to 1.18 mm ) and adjust side check nuts in a 4:1 ratio .
5. What rotates the differential pinion shafts? (Short Answer)
Answer: The differential carrier (cage assembly) , which is directly turned by the crown gear .
6. To which gear are the half shaft splines connected? (Short Answer)
Answer: The external splines of the half axle shafts connect to the internal splines of the Sun Gears (Side Gears) .
7. What kind of joint is used to link the differential with the shaft, and why? (Short Answer)
Answer: A Universal Joint (U-joint) with companion flange is used — to permit continuous rotary power transmission at changing operational angles caused by rear axle suspension movement.
8. What lubricants are commonly used in a differential? (Short Answer)
Answer: Extreme-Pressure (EP) Hypoid Gear Oil — e.g., SAE 80W-90, SAE 90, or SAE 140 API GL-5.
9. The differential allows wheels to rotate at _____ speeds when a vehicle turns.
Answer: Different
10. The primary function of the differential is to transfer engine power to the _____.
Answer: Wheels / drive axles
11. Proper adjustment of backlash ensures correct tooth _____ between gears.
Answer: Contact / engagement
12. In the differential, the components responsible for transmitting torque to the wheels are the half axle _____.
Answer: Shafts
13. What is the main purpose of a differential in a vehicle? (MCQ)
Answer: (C) To allow wheels to rotate at different speeds during turns
14. Which of the following gears is NOT a part of a differential? (MCQ)
Answer: (D) Drive gear
15. The differential's job includes: (MCQ)
Answer: (C) Transferring power to the wheels and allowing speed variation during turns
16. When a vehicle turns, which wheels travel a shorter distance? (MCQ)
Answer: (B) Rear wheels (Note: the inner wheels follow a shorter path than the outer wheels.)
17. List the functions performed by a differential. (Activity)
S.No. Function
1 Converts longitudinal propeller shaft rotation to transverse wheel rotation (90° drive transfer).
2 Provides final speed reduction to amplify torque at the driving wheels.
3 Allows the outer wheel to rotate faster than the inner wheel during turns.
4 Distributes equal torque to both drive wheels during straight-line travel.
18. Write the steps to overhaul the differential unit and carry out adjustments. (Activity)
S.No. Step / Procedure
1 Drain gear oil, disconnect propeller shaft flange, and pull out both half axle shafts.
2 Remove side caps and pull out crown wheel, star gears, sun gears and pinion assembly.
3 Clean all parts in kerosene, inspect gear teeth for cracks/chipping, and replace worn gears and brass thrust washers.
4 Reassemble gears using shims, apply red oxide paste to verify tooth contact, and adjust pinion position.
5 Set up a dial gauge to check backlash (0.15–1.18 mm for crown/pinion; 0.10–0.20 mm for sun/star gears) and adjust side check nuts to specification.

Key Points & Facts

  • Differential: 90° drive transfer + different wheel speeds on turns.
  • Main Parts: Crown wheel, drive pinion, star gears, sun gears, carrier, half shafts.
  • Half Shafts: Connect to Sun Gears (side gears).
  • Carrier: Rotated directly by the crown gear.
  • Backlash: 0.15–1.18 mm (crown/pinion); 0.10–0.20 mm (sun/star).
  • Lubricant: EP Hypoid gear oil (API GL-5, SAE 80W-90/90/140).
  • U-Joint: Links differential to shaft for changing angles.
